# THE HARVESTER'S SKY
## A Murder Mystery Dinner Game

---

## PROJECT OVERVIEW

A murder mystery game set in 1274 CE at the court of Ilkhan Abaqa in Tabriz, blending:
- **Historical intrigue** — Mongol-Papal diplomatic tensions, the Franco-Mongol alliance attempts against the Mamluks
- **Cosmic horror** — Lovecraftian Old Gods, the Sleepers in the Wheel
- **Ancient mystery** — Scythian burial mounds, Tengrist shamanism, disturbed artifacts

## CORE CONCEPT

### The Surface Mystery
A murder at the Mongol court during alliance negotiations. Political suspects, religious tensions, trade rivalries.

### The Deep Mystery
The victim was a "node" — someone whose death weakens multiple egregores simultaneously. The killing follows ancient Scythian sacrificial patterns. A golden artifact from a disturbed kurgan has arrived at court. Someone is thinning the membrane between humanity and the Sleepers.

---

## KEY THEMES

1. **The collision of civilizations** — East meets West, Christianity meets Tengrism
2. **The weight of history** — Ancient lands soaked in blood, buried things that wake
3. **The fragility of belief** — Egregores that protect humanity can be starved and killed
4. **Cosmic indifference** — The Sleepers don't hate us; they simply are
